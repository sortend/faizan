Healthcare · Multi-page web site

Lumen Health Institute

A complete hospital and quaternary-care institute site — built as real information architecture, not a single landing page. Doctor directory, ten departments, health checkup packages, and an appointment flow, all tied together.

Visit live site ↗

Overview

Lumen Health needed a site that could hold the actual scale of a hospital: dozens of doctors, ten clinical departments, diagnostic packages, a blog, and a patient portal — while still opening with a hero that felt calm and human rather than corporate.

The build leans on a clear navigation structure and repeatable page templates, so adding a new doctor or department doesn't mean redesigning a page from scratch.

Key features

  • Ten department pages — cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopedics, pediatrics, and more
  • Doctor directory with specialty, credentials, and same-day availability
  • Appointment booking flow and a dedicated 24/7 emergency page
  • Health checkup packages with pricing and what's included
  • Patient portal, health blog, and full facility gallery
  • Patient testimonials and clear contact / emergency contact info

E-commerce · Storefront + admin panel

Mobile Gallery

A real mobile phone shop in Simrahi Bazar, Bihar, needed a storefront and a way to run it themselves. This is a full retail site — product catalog, pre-orders with UPI payment, customer reviews — paired with a self-serve admin panel.

Visit live site ↗

Overview

The brief was a working store, not just a brochure: customers needed to browse phones and accessories by brand, place a pre-order and pay a deposit through UPI, and leave reviews — while the shop owner needed to manage every bit of it without touching code.

That split shaped the build: a public storefront focused on speed and trust signals, and a locked admin panel behind login that mirrors every editable piece of the public site.

Key features

  • Phone & accessory catalog with brand filters and product photos
  • Pre-order flow with UPI QR code payment, paid directly to the owner's account
  • Customer accounts, order history, and a review system
  • Full admin dashboard — add/edit phones, brands, gadgets, events, and gallery photos
  • Editable hero section and store info, so the owner controls the homepage copy
  • WhatsApp and call-to-store contact built into every product page

Hospitality · Brand-led multi-page site

Shahi Darbar

A multi-cuisine restaurant, hotel, and marriage hall on NH27 needed a site that felt as premium as the venue itself — a royal, marble-and-gold visual identity, real photography, a heritage story, and a full menu.

Visit live site ↗

Overview

Shahi Darbar is three businesses in one — a multi-cuisine restaurant, a hotel, and a marriage hall that hosts weddings for up to 500 guests. The site is organized around "three ways to be welcomed": fine dining, the hotel, and banquets — each with its own moment on the page.

Real interior and facade photography carries most of the persuasion, paired with a heritage narrative and a signature menu built from the restaurant's actual dishes.

Key features

  • Heritage story section establishing the restaurant, hotel, and banquet hall as one landmark
  • Signature menu across three cuisines, pulled from the real kitchen
  • Dedicated photo gallery and guest-reviews pages
  • Marriage hall / banquet section built to sell wedding bookings specifically
  • Map embed, call, WhatsApp, and social links in the contact section
  • Admin login page for the owner to manage site content

Productivity · Client-side web app

StudentHub

A student productivity toolkit that proves a small, well-designed feature set beats a bloated one: a to-do list, a notes pad, and a Pomodoro study timer, in one calm page with zero sign-up.

Visit live site ↗

Overview

StudentHub is deliberately small. The goal wasn't another all-in-one student app with logins and social features — it was to see how far three focused tools could go on their own, with everything saved locally in the browser.

No frameworks, no dependencies — just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which kept the whole thing fast and easy to reason about.

Key features

  • To-do list with auto-save, task counts, and clear-completed
  • Quick notes pad for lecture thoughts and reminders
  • Pomodoro-style study timer — 25-minute focus, 5-minute short break, 15-minute long break
  • Curated study resources list — notes templates, open textbooks, tutorials
  • Dark mode toggle and a distraction-free single-page layout
  • All data stored locally via localStorage — no account required
